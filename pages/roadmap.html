Rules for everything on this roadmap

Nothing ships unless it keeps these true.

No build

Plain CSS, JS and SVG. Works from a folder.

Token-driven

Colours, timing and radius come from theme variables.

Accessible and fast

Reduced motion honoured, no layout jank, small payload.
